# Product notes

A student can solve a question correctly and still walk away thinking they got it wrong. That costs time, especially if they redo the problem to figure out what they missed. I put grading errors near the top of the queue so faculty can get to them sooner. The proposed change sits beside the evidence so the reviewer can check the reasoning without opening another screen.

I would never let the system invent a missing figure or write a distractor on its own. In R009, the physics gives us 10 m/s and confirms option C. It tells us nothing about what option D used to say. I would send that case to faculty with the missing option flagged. In this build I also leave answer-key changes for faculty to approve.

In week one I would track how often faculty approve a patch without editing it. I'd look at that number by repair type. If faculty keep rewriting a certain kind of patch, I'd look at those cases before generating more of them.

I'd pitch a way for faculty to take a confirmed broken question out of new practice sessions while they fix it. Students could stop hitting a bad key as soon as the defect is confirmed.
